How RoomRaah works

Every listing here has been through the same four checks, and every conversation stays on the platform so there is a record of what was agreed.

  1. Search without an account

    Browse rooms, open a listing, look through the photographs and read the reviews. None of that asks you to sign up. Filter by city, area, rent, room type or how far it is from your university or workplace.

  2. Check what we checked

    Each listing shows what has been verified — the owner’s identity, the address, the photographs, the facilities and how many beds are actually free. A listing with an inspection badge has been visited by somebody from RoomRaah.

  3. Arrange a visit

    Verify your email, then request a visit — in person or over video — and pick a time. The owner accepts or declines, and if they decline they have to say why. There are no telephone numbers on RoomRaah: messages and visits go through the platform so that what was promised is written down.

  4. Review it afterwards

    Once a visit has happened you can review the listing. Reviews are read by a moderator before they appear, which is why a new one is not visible straight away.

Have a room to let?

Create an owner account, add your listing with three to fifteen photographs, and send it for review. Once it is published, any edit you make is reviewed before it goes live — so what a seeker reads is always something somebody checked. Free beds are their own, faster confirmation, so availability never goes stale.
